A Letter to Students

Dear Student,

When I came to the UK to study in 2002, I wanted to see what the world looked like beyond the one I knew. I could not have imagined how much that journey would change my understanding of education, other people and myself.

Years later, as a university teacher, I have seen students work incredibly hard, sometimes achieving excellent results, yet still feeling uncertain about their abilities or what comes next.

These experiences have made me ask a question: What should education leave us able to do?

Today, as AI increasingly helps us understand ideas, produce work and make decisions, this question matters more than ever.

I believe education should give us more than knowledge and qualifications. It should help us develop the judgement to make decisions, the confidence to act, and the ability to keep learning when circumstances change.

That is why I wrote Studying in the UK and began developing this website. I hope the ideas and activities here will help you explore your own learning, question what you encounter and find your way forward.

Wherever your journey takes you, I hope you leave education with something that remains yours.

With warm wishes,

Qiaoyuan Lin
