Finding Direction

Sometimes you are studying but feel uncertain about where you are going, why your work matters or how to move forward. You may have a clear goal and still find it difficult to act. These resources help you recognise what is happening, consider what matters to you and choose a practical next step. Start with whichever problem feels relevant.

Which problem are you facing?
