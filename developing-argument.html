Developing an Argument

You may understand the topic and have collected useful reading, yet still struggle to develop an argument of your own. These resources help you make a clear claim, use evidence purposefully, consider other explanations and reach a judgement you can explain and defend. They are particularly useful for open-ended assessments, but the skills also matter in presentations, reports and discussions. Start with the problem that best matches your situation.

Which problem are you facing?

PROBLEM 01

I’ve read widely, but I don’t know what my argument is.

Move from collecting other people’s ideas to identifying the question and a position worth examining.

PROBLEM 02

My work describes ideas rather than evaluating them.

PROBLEM 04

Different sources disagree. How should I respond?

Compare competing explanations and assess their relevance, evidence and limitations rather than listing them side by side.

PROBLEM 05
