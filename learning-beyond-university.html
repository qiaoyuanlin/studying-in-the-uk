PRACTICAL LEARNING · AREA 04

Learning Beyond University

Completing a degree is an important achievement. But what knowledge, ways of thinking and abilities might you carry into situations beyond university? These resources invite you to identify what you have learned, test it in unfamiliar contexts and consider your next steps. Recognising a skill is a starting point; using it in a new situation takes practice. Start with the question that matters to you now.

Which problem are you facing?

Look at real learning experiences and identify the knowledge, abilities and ways of thinking you have practised.
